The Vesper Bond series

Sometimes a person enters your life by accident. And then it turns out you'll never be the same after them.

Vesper and Vinculum are two books in one story about an encounter that gradually changes everything: relationships, decisions, boundaries, and what two people can even be to each other.

This isn't a simple love story. There's a past that catches up, decisions that have to be paid for, and a bond that becomes too strong to call an accident.

"Some places choose people long before people choose them."
