About

Lina Krivenko

Novelist, philosophy student and influencer marketer.

I was born in Krasnoyarsk, a city where winter lasts half the year and you learn to treasure every warm day. I spent several years in Yekaterinburg, then took a big leap and moved to Belgrade. The city turned out to be exactly what it should be: loud, warm and a little chaotic.

I write books. It didn’t start as a plan but as a necessity: the stories simply wouldn’t fit inside me and asked to be put on paper. Today I have three novels, each one a separate world with its own atmosphere and characters who sometimes behave nothing like I intended.

I’m also studying philosophy, and, oddly enough, it helps my writing a lot. When you constantly think about the nature of things and the meaning of words, you start building sentences differently.

I work in influencer marketing, and it’s not just a profession, it’s a love of its own. Advertising, when it’s done honestly, is an art: finding the person who speaks to an audience in the same language, and shaping a story so that it doesn’t sell but hooks. I help brands and authors tell their story through bloggers and social media, so that it sounds natural and lands exactly with their people. I love this balance between analytics and creativity, between numbers and real emotions.

I study foreign languages, because every language is a different way of thinking. I dance. In the evenings I colour in pictures when I need to switch my head off and simply be.

I love summer, warm seas and iced coffee, preferably all at once. I adore cats; I don’t have one yet, but that’s temporary. I follow hockey and sincerely dream of being at an NHL game one day, in person, with a hot dog and a huge scarf of my favourite team.
